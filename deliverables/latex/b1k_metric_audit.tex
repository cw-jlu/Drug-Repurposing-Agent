\section{B1k official NS-AUC
diagnostic}\label{b1k-official-ns-auc-diagnostic}

Status: \textbf{all 100 previously scored official seeds per split} have
now been reproduced within absolute tolerance \texttt{1e-12} using the
frozen seed/result CSVs, identical splitter, five-fold B1k selection,
and official rowwise NS-AUC calculation. The
\href{../benchmark/results/b1k_all_seeds_random.json}{random-simple
report},
\href{../benchmark/results/b1k_all_seeds_weak.json}{weakly-correlated
report}, per-seed JSONL checkpoints, chained traces and
\href{../benchmark/results/analytic_audits_archive_receipt.json}{archive
receipt} are versioned. The official CSVs and primary 100-run comparison
are unchanged. Reproduce with
\texttt{python\ -m\ evals.\allowbreak{}audit\_\allowbreak{}b1k\_\allowbreak{}all\_\allowbreak{}seeds\ -\/\allowbreak{}-split\ random\_\allowbreak{}simple\ -\/\allowbreak{}-output\ artifacts/\allowbreak{}reports/\allowbreak{}b1k\_\allowbreak{}all\_\allowbreak{}seeds\_\allowbreak{}random.\allowbreak{}json}
and the analogous \texttt{weakly\_\allowbreak{}correlated} command using a new output
path. Each run checks source-data, frozen seed, and frozen-result hashes
and refuses output overwrite.

The pinned runner calls
\texttt{benchscofi.\allowbreak{}utils.\allowbreak{}rowwise\_\allowbreak{}metrics.\allowbreak{}calc\_\allowbreak{}auc} with
\texttt{transpose=False}, so NS-AUC is an \textbf{unweighted mean over
eligible drug rows}. Its comparison is strictly
\texttt{positive\_\allowbreak{}score\ \textgreater{}\ nonpositive\_\allowbreak{}score}; an exact
tie contributes zero, unlike standard AUC\textquotesingle s half credit.
Rows without both labels are omitted. The official global AUC and
earlier project-level AUC use different aggregation/score handling and
must not be used to infer a reversed sign.

All 200 seeds have a majority of eligible positive--nonpositive pairs
tied. The B1k neighbor-label propagation generates many identical scores
in this sparse association matrix, and the pinned strict comparison
awards those ties zero. \textbf{Tie handling is therefore the dominant
arithmetic reason for B1k\textquotesingle s low official NS-AUC across
these seeds}; simply inverting score direction would make it worse. The
half-tie figures are counterfactual diagnostics on B1k\textquotesingle s
saved-score reproduction, \textbf{not} new official benchmark scores,
not commensurate with other methods\textquotesingle{} untouched official
scores, and not a justification to revise the comparator after viewing
results. Replacing the metric would require rerunning all our methods
and the 11 published baselines under one prospective definition. This
retrospective audit does not establish clinical prediction value.

and

reports remain as initial diagnostics; the all-seed reports supersede
any extrapolation from only those two cases.
