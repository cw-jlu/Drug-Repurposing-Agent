## Shared pathway evidence and contradiction

Nine of ten names are known or likely glucocorticoids; BRD-K84203638 is unresolved. This explains a coherent transcriptional class signal, but it also means the Top-10 is not ten independent mechanistic hypotheses.

- Supporting preclinical context: dexamethasone suppressed A549 and Calu-1 proliferation through ERK/MAPK and cell-cycle regulation ([PMID 12204894](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12204894/)). A biomarker-selected study found dexamethasone responses in STK11/LKB1-mutant NSCLC models ([PMID 37035141](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37035141/)). These findings are class-level and biomarker-dependent; they do not validate the nine screened compounds.
- Contradicting preclinical context: dexamethasone reduced pemetrexed cytotoxicity in GR-responsive nonsquamous NSCLC models ([PMID 24736075](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24736075/)). A later study linked GR/β-catenin activation to lung-cancer stemness, chemoresistance, worse survival signals, and reduced paclitaxel benefit ([PMID 41344105](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41344105/)).
- Clinical-context uncertainty: observational inhaled-corticosteroid studies sometimes report lower lung-cancer incidence, but a systematic review found no significant effect in the included randomized trials ([PMID 27761973](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27761973/)); a later meta-analysis rated the evidence low to very low ([PMID 35843928](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35843928/)). This is prevention evidence in COPD/asthma populations, not LUAD treatment evidence.
- Immunotherapy context: retrospective NSCLC studies associate baseline or early systemic steroids with worse checkpoint-inhibitor outcomes, although indication-related confounding is material and some analyses differ ([PMID 33007977](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33007977/), [PMID 33431285](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33431285/), [PMID 36142204](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36142204/)).

## Bottom line

The reconstruction resolves the exact source signature and perturbagen IDs, but it does not produce a clinically credible Top-10. Cross-source formulation or stereochemistry conflicts remain for several rows. Only hydrocortisone and beclomethasone dipropionate have candidate-specific A549/NSCLC context, and neither has candidate-specific LUAD efficacy evidence. The appropriate output remains a hypothesis list with `insufficient_evidence`. The prespecified dose, subtype, mechanism and combination plan is in `docs/luad_experimental_validation_protocol.md`.

The search was deliberately bounded to exact names, likely formulation synonyms, A549/LUAD/NSCLC terms, and shared glucocorticoid evidence. “No study found” means none was identified in this bounded review, not proof that no literature exists.
